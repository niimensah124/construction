01 Our Story

Construction is built by people.

Eajek Construction Ltd was created around a simple idea: finding the right people for a construction project shouldn't have to be difficult.

Building projects require more than materials and plans. They require skilled hands, experienced workers and people who understand their craft.

From masons and labourers to electricians, plumbers, carpenters, welders, painters and finishing professionals, we help clients connect with the people their project requires.
